Career progression

The System Administrator pathway

System administration careers grow through hands-on experience, expanding technical knowledge, and increasing responsibility.

01
Entry level

IT Support Technician

Provides first-line technical support and resolves everyday hardware, software, and account issues.

  • Responsibilities: Ticket handling, device setup, password resets, and basic troubleshooting.
  • Skills: Windows, customer service, networking basics, and active directory fundamentals.
02
Intermediate

System Administrator

Maintains servers, networks, users, and data services while keeping organizational systems secure and available.

  • Responsibilities: Server management, backups, user access, patching, and documentation.
  • Skills: 1–3 years of experience, scripting, virtualization, security, and cloud familiarity.
03
Advanced

Systems Engineer

Designs, automates, and improves complex infrastructure across on-premises and cloud environments.

  • Responsibilities: Architecture, automation, disaster recovery, performance, and strategic planning.
  • Skills: Advanced networking, cloud platforms, automation, leadership, and project experience.

Foundations for career growth

Build these core abilities through coursework, labs, certifications, and real-world practice.

Operating Systems

Administer Windows and Linux systems, including users, services, permissions, and updates.

Understand DNS, DHCP, routing, firewalls, and TCP/IP to diagnose and maintain connectivity.

⚙

◇

Job roles

Where system expertise makes an impact

HD

Help Desk Technician

The primary point of contact for technology problems. This role focuses on users and immediate issue resolution.

  • Diagnoses hardware and software issues
  • Documents tickets and solutions
  • Escalates complex incidents
SA

System Administrator

The operational owner of servers and infrastructure, keeping systems available, secure, and properly configured.

  • Manages accounts, servers, and backups
  • Applies updates and security controls
  • Performs monitoring and recovery
SE

Systems Engineer

The strategic technical designer responsible for scalable systems, automation, and complex infrastructure projects.

  • Plans and designs infrastructure
  • Builds automation and integrations
  • Leads modernization initiatives
